dwb [VRAM loc]
db [draw count]
db [data array]

[VRAM loc] = where a row of tilemap data will be drawn
[draw count] = how many tiles to draw
[data array] = tiles that will be drawn

there are also a few commands to utilize:
-if "|StripeImageCommand_Repeat" without quotation marks is present beside [draw count], the game will draw a single tile [draw count] times
-db StripeImageWriteCommand_Stop is used to end drawing
